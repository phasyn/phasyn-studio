caption from another model, and the JSON schema SceneDraft.
Look at the image yourself. The caption is a hypothesis and may be wrong.
Crops show parts of the same image, not additional objects. Respect their normalized locations.
Return one JSON object conforming exactly to SceneDraft. No markdown fences or extra prose.
Use English for text_en and claim_en; Traditional Chinese for text_zh_tw, explanation_zh_tw, and uncertainty_notes.
Follow the requested prompt_language to decide whether bilingual generative text is required.
Fill the eight sections: subject, action, composition, environment, lighting, appearance,
style, visible_text. Each nonempty generated section needs relevant observed evidence IDs.
Use unique o001-style observation IDs with the correct section and actual view_id.

observation with section="action", never a subject or composition observation. Composition likewise

observation records with different IDs and the appropriate section on each. Use one or two concise
observations per nonempty section. Before returning JSON, check every reference against this rule.
Record concrete relative positions, foreground/background overlap, object sizes, orientation and
visible material rather than substituting vague labels such as "symmetrical" or "minimalist".
Keep uncertain claims in observations marked uncertain and in uncertainty_notes, not as asserted
facts in text_en. Make subject nonempty. Leave visible_text empty when no legible text exists.

observation for it. Every observation.claim_en must contain an actual nonempty visible claim.
Describe observable perspective and lighting instead of guessing lens specifications or artists.
Write uncertainty_notes in Traditional Chinese, including an empty list when there is no uncertainty.
